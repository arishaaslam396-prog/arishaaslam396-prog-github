Industrial Machine Learning & Supply Chain Automation

Prototype

A machine-learning prototype designed to estimate food inventory waste risk using product category, storage temperature, humidity, shelf life and storage duration. The proposed system supports proactive FEFO decisions.

ModelRandom Forest Regressor
TargetWaste Risk Score (0–100%)
PipelinePreprocessing → Training → Evaluation → Prediction
DeploymentStreamlit dashboard

Key Inputs

  • Storage temperature
  • Storage humidity
  • Shelf-life days
  • Days in storage
  • Product category
  • Item name
